# Module 1 Project: AI Test Results

* **Business:** Sweet Crumbs, bakery, Lahore
* **Model:** llama3.2

## Prompt 1: Marketing
* **Prompt:** Write a short Instagram post for Sweet Crumbs, a bakery in Lahore. Target university students. Friendly tone, 1 call to action.
* **AI Answer:** Craving something sweet between classes? 🍰 Drop by Sweet Crumbs in Lahore for the freshest treats in town! Show your student ID for a special discount today. Tag your study buddy below! 👇
* **Useful?** Yes, because it hit all constraints: friendly tone, targeted university students, and included a clear call to action.

## Prompt 2: Customer Service
* **Prompt:** You are the manager of Sweet Crumbs. Write a polite reply to a customer whose order arrived late. Apologise and offer a small discount.
* **AI Answer:** Dear Customer, We sincerely apologize that your order from Sweet Crumbs arrived later than expected. We know how much you were looking forward to it. As a makeup, we've attached a 15% discount code for your next treat. Thank you for your patience!
* **Useful?** Yes, because it maintained a professional, apologetic tone and followed the instruction to offer a practical discount.

## Prompt 3: Growth
* **Prompt:** Suggest 3 low-cost AI tools a bakery in Lahore can use to attract university students with a budget of 15000 PKR per month.
* **AI Answer:**
1. Canva AI / Magic Studio: Free/low-cost social media graphics to catch student eyes.
2. ChatGPT / Claude Free Tiers: To brainstorm weekly caption calendars and student promo ideas.
3. Meta Ads Manager (AI Targeting): To run hyper-local Instagram ads targeted specifically around university campuses within your 15,000 PKR budget.
* **Useful?** Yes, the tools suggested fit comfortably within the 15,000 PKR monthly limit and directly addressed local student engagement.
